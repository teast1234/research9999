### <mark style="background-color:#ffd591">⚠️ THE GROUND TRUTH — Hormuz is a trickle, and diplomacy is dead</mark> 🟩

- 🟩 **The MOU expired, talks stalled:** The interim ceasefire memorandum of understanding between the U.S. and Iran has now **expired**, with negotiations to reopen the Strait of Hormuz still stalled, keeping oil elevated.

- 🟡 🟩 **The US claims the upper hand:** At the moment the evidence is clear: The United States, patrolling the strait with its navy, is gaining ground — and Iran is losing much of its control of the critical waterway. **More than 80%** of liquids transiting over the past two weeks have taken the **Omani route** — a UN-authorized shipping channel that Iran vehemently opposes.

- 🟩 **The "dark transit" workaround:** Aided by the US Navy, oil companies have chartered tankers to **turn their transponders off** and shuttle oil out of the Persian Gulf, through the Strait of Hormuz, to the Gulf of Oman, where they offload their crude to waiting tankers.

- 🔴 🟩 **Iran tightening the screws on shippers:** Iran's state-controlled Persian Gulf Strait Authority said vessels that violate what it described as Iranian arrangements could face **"fines, seizure, or confiscation"** during future passages. *"Vessels violating Iranian protocols for the Strait of Hormuz shall face restrictions on future passages, including fines, detention, or confiscation."*

- 🟩 **Selective access as leverage:** Iran is allowing a number of **Iraqi oil tankers** to pass through the Strait of Hormuz, in the latest example of Tehran granting selective access to the vital waterway.
